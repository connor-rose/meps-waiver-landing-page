Frequently asked questions

What is a mental health waiver evaluation?

It is an independent report used to provide MEPS with a determination of a candidate's ability to perform in the military. It is based on their mental health history as well as current level of functioning.

How much does it cost?

The fee is a flat $400. It includes the record review, the one-hour telehealth evaluation, the written report, and a follow-up evaluation if MEPS requests one.

How long does it take?

The evaluation is one hour by telehealth. Your report is delivered within 3–7 business days of the completed interview.

Do you guarantee a waiver approval?

No. The waiver decision is made by the military service, not by me. I commit to a fair and accurate report that focuses on what the waiver authority needs to know.

Who can request an evaluation?

Candidates from any branch can request an appointment themselves. Recruiters are welcome to contact me to schedule one for a candidate. Not sure you need a waiver? Ask your recruiter or MEPS first.

What will I need to provide?

Any applicable original medical documents related to your mental health disqualification, plus your MEPS document showing which specific 6.28.X standard was considered disqualifying. If anything is missing, email me before your appointment.

Where are evaluations held, and who can you see?

All evaluations are by telehealth. I am licensed in Virginia, and my PSYPACT membership is pending. PSYPACT, the Psychology Interjurisdictional Compact, lets a psychologist licensed in one member state provide telehealth to people located in other member states without a separate license in each.

Until it is approved, I can see candidates who are located in Virginia at the time of the evaluation. Once approved, I will be able to see candidates in other PSYPACT states too. Not sure where you stand? Email me before scheduling.

Who sees the report?

Your report is delivered directly to you upon completion.

What if MEPS asks for more information?

A follow-up evaluation is included in the $400 fee at no additional charge.
